# Privacy Policy

**This is a placeholder draft, not reviewed by counsel.** It's structured to cover what a payment processor's business-profile verification checks for (what's collected, how it's used, who it's shared with and how, and what security practices are in place), but the specific commitments below need a real legal review before this is treated as binding — edit this file directly (any text editor, no code involved) once that review is done.

## What We Collect

DVURE collects the information you provide when you create an account or use the platform: your name, email address, and role (brand, agency, model, or crew); information about your organization; and, for models and crew, profile details such as measurements, day rates, and portfolio images submitted for casting consideration.

When a payment is made through DVURE, payment card details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor, Stripe — DVURE does not receive or store full card numbers.

## How We Use It

Information is used to operate the platform: matching talent to campaigns, coordinating bookings and call sheets, and processing and tracking payments between brands, agencies, models, and crew.

## Who It's Shared With

Campaign and casting information is shared with the other parties involved in a given booking (for example, a brand and the agencies it invites to submit talent). Payment information is shared with Stripe to process charges and payouts. DVURE does not sell personal information to third parties.

## Security

Access to account data is restricted by role — an organization only sees data tied to its own campaigns, bookings, and relationships, enforced at the database level. Payment card data is handled entirely by Stripe under its own PCI-compliant infrastructure.

## Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to support@dvure.com.
